🚨 Fraud Investigation Report

Deconstructing the "Innovators & Company" Demat Job Scam

Modus Operandi

This case study exposes a widespread social engineering scam operating under the domain innovatorsofficials.com. The perpetrators use fake HR profiles to offer "Work From Home" telecalling jobs. Their ultimate goal is not employment, but forcing candidates to open Demat Accounts to generate unauthorized affiliate commissions or create mule accounts.

Evidence 1: Front-End Recruiter ( samaira saifi)

Status: Scripted responses, fake urgency, TL Code: INV-91339

The scam begins with unsolicited messages from unverified WhatsApp Business accounts claiming to have found the candidate on Indeed.

Evidence 2: Second Recruiter (khushimohabia)

Status: Identical script pattern, TL Code: INV-96679, Zoom Host: Alok Sir

When the first attempt fails or stalls, the network routes the candidate to a second "recruiter" using the exact same copy-pasted templates, proving this is a coordinated operation.

Evidence 3: OSINT & Infrastructure Analysis

Technical lookups reveal that the main operator behind the virtual numbers is flagged as a scammer, and the website infrastructure is newly registered on a cheap shared host.

Evidence 4: Fake Portal & Decoy Location

The dashboard contains hidden affiliate links designed to steal referral payouts. Furthermore, the physical address provided points to a random metro station to create false legitimacy.

Compiled for educational and cybersecurity awareness purposes. Stay safe online.
